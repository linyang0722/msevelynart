关于我

I've studied art since middle school, trained in fashion design & engineering and fine art, and earned an MBA in the U.S. — so I see education through the eyes of a creator and a manager.

Over 12 years in U.S. private and public schools I have taught, designed curricula, and led programs. I founded and ran my own Montessori school and art studio for six years — handling everything from setup and management to sales and enrollment.

Today I lead online marketing and enrollment for art and Montessori schools, build their websites and social channels, and design the visuals and videos myself. I'm especially strong at connecting with Chinese-speaking families and guiding students toward top U.S. art schools.

我从中学开始学习艺术，本科服装设计与工程，纯艺术进修两年，美国MBA硕士。12年在美国公私立学校执教与办学：从创办、管理到招生，一手打造自己的蒙台梭利学校和艺术工作室。我擅长把艺术、教育和营销结合在一起，尤其擅长服务华人家庭。
